01

The Emergency Department is losing the race

/A

Time is tissue

A STEMI's cath-lab door-to-balloon window is 90 minutes. Every minute of delay raises mortality. Triage is where those minutes are won — or lost.

ACS mortality ↑ 7.5% / 30-min delay
/B

Overwhelmed clinicians

Emergency departments are overcrowded and understaffed. One mass-casualty event can drop 5 critical patients on a team built for one.

ED boarding ↑ 300% since 2010
/C

Protocols fail under pressure

Sepsis needs antibiotics within the 3-hour bundle. Stroke needs a CT within 25 minutes. Gaps between guideline and execution are where patients are lost.

Only ~50% of codes achieve target times

SURGICAL asks: can an AI agent be trained to follow these protocols flawlessly, at speed, under scarcity — and teach humans to do the same? It is a safe training ground for emergency triage decision-making.

      Under the hood

      The agent loop

      Observe→ Decide→ Act→ Reward→ Reassess

      The copilot reads live vitals, picks the next critical action from a clinical policy (optionally driven by a frontier LLM — DeepSeek-V3.2 on Featherless), executes it, and learns from the reward signal — ESI accuracy, protocol timing, disposition safety. Every action carries a human-readable rationale.
