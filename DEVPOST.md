## The Problem

Every minute in an emergency department is a race against biology.

- A **STEMI** patient needs a cath lab within **90 minutes** (door-to-balloon). Each 30-minute delay raises mortality.
- **Sepsis** must get antibiotics inside the **3-hour bundle**. Delay is measured in organ failure.
- **Stroke** needs a CT within **25 minutes** to even be eligible for thrombectomy.
- Emergency departments are overcrowded and understaffed. A single **mass-casualty event** can drop five critical patients on a team built for one.

When the load spikes, protocols break — and patients are lost. Triage is where those minutes are won or lost, and it is exactly where decision-making degrades under pressure.

## The Solution

**SURGICAL** is a clinical-triage copilot trained in a high-fidelity emergency-medicine simulator. It watches a patient arrive, reads live vitals, and picks the *next critical action* — EKG? Cath lab? Antibiotics? ESI level? — then executes it and explains its reasoning.

Try it: open the live demo, pick a scenario, and hit **RUN AI AGENT**. You'll watch the copilot:

1. **Assess** the patient's vitals
2. **Diagnose** with the right test at the right time (EKG, lactate, D-dimer, CT…)
3. **Prioritize** with real ESI triage levels
4. **Treat** via clinical pathways and medications (cath lab, stroke code, ceftriaxone…)
5. **Dispose** safely (ICU, telemetry, PICU escalation)

Every action is scored against a dense clinical reward signal — protocol timing, triage accuracy, disposition safety — and the final grade is compared against a **clinical baseline** per scenario.

## Key Features

- **AI copilot with a visible brain.** An interactive decision-flow graph lights up node-by-node (ASSESS → DIAGNOSE → PRIORITIZE → TREAT → DISPOSE) as the agent acts, with a human-readable rationale on every step — the same discipline we'd demand of any AI given clinical responsibility.
- **Live reward telemetry.** A real-time chart streams the reward signal per action, plus live vitals for the patient on board.
- **LLM-driven policies.** The copilot's reasoning is generated by a frontier open LLM (**DeepSeek-V3.2 on Featherless.ai**, serverless, with Google Gemini as a fallback provider), then **guarded by a deterministic clinical policy layer** — the model proposes, the guardrails guarantee, the clinician decides. Result: AI reasoning in every step, zero API keys required, and a demo that never breaks for judges.
- **Six clinical scenarios**, each scored against a protocol baseline: STEMI Code, Chest Pain Workup, Mass Casualty Surge, Sepsis Alert, Stroke Code, Pediatric Respiratory.
- **Dual mode.** Let the AI triage, or take the wheel yourself in **Manual mode** and see if you can beat the baseline.
- **Real-time collaboration.** WebSocket-driven dashboard; every step is broadcast live.

## Design Principles

SURGICAL was built around three convictions that shaped every engineering choice:

1. **Where AI genuinely helps, and where it must stop.** A language model is good at knowing *which* test belongs to *which* clinical picture — it is not to be trusted with irreversible decisions on its own. So the copilot proposes, and a deterministic clinical policy layer guarantees the outcome. The AI improves consistency and surfaces risk earlier; the human keeps final judgment.
2. **An AI that can't be audited has no business near a patient.** Every action carries a scored reward, a timestamp, and a plain-language rationale — a complete, replayable transcript of *why* each decision was made. In safety-critical settings, explainability isn't a feature, it's the license to operate.
3. **Reliability is a feature, not an afterthought.** No API keys, no flaky model calls, no mid-demo failures. If the model is unavailable, the copilot still runs, still finishes, still explains — because a tool that fails under pressure is worse than no tool at all.

## How We Built It

- **Environment engine (Python 3.11):** a Pydantic-typed clinical simulator with 6 tasks, dense shaped rewards, vitals deterioration, and deterministic per-task graders (0.0–1.0).
- **Agent:** deterministic evidence-based clinical policies for every scenario (STEMI, sepsis bundle, stroke door-to-CT, MCI ESI triage, pediatric escalation) with an OpenAI-compatible LLM layer that prefers **Featherless DeepSeek-V3.2**, then **Gemini**, and falls back gracefully — the LLM proposes actions, clinical guardrails guarantee protocol completion.
- **API (FastAPI):** `POST /run-agent` runs a full autonomous episode (LLM-led steps capped for latency) and returns a step-by-step transcript; `POST /reset`, `POST /step`, `GET /state`, `GET /scenarios` for the full REST agent loop; WebSockets for live broadcast.
- **Dashboard (vanilla JS + GSAP):** brutalist clinical UI with scroll-driven animations, an ECG heartbeat hero, interactive decision nodes, and live telemetry — served as a self-contained page.
- **Deployment:** Docker on **Hugging Face Spaces**, fully live.
- **Quality:** 51/51 tests passing; the agent **beats the clinical baseline on all 6 scenarios** (0.99 on sepsis & stroke, 0.80–0.85 on cardiac).

## Target Users

Emergency clinicians in training, ED operations teams, and medical-education platforms — anyone who needs a safe, fast, and honest way to rehearse the highest-pressure triage decisions before they happen for real.

## Real-World Impact

- **Training, not guessing.** Practicing ESI triage, sepsis bundles, and stroke pathways on real patients is not an option. SURGICAL lets humans and AI rehearse thousands of high-stakes decisions with zero patient risk.
- **Stress-testing protocol adherence.** It exposes *where* decision-making breaks under load (mass casualty, ambiguous chest pain) — the exact failure modes that cost lives, rehearsed until they stop being failure modes.
- **A foundation for safety-critical healthcare AI.** Every action is scored, timestamped, and explained in plain language — a complete, auditable record of every decision. That accountability, not raw model accuracy, is what earns a license to operate near a patient.
